;<h1/>Example for changing the slave No. of a PLC link via a ladder
;Note: Slave No. can be set up directly in the Unit Editor. 
;Slave No. of the serial PLC link of Port1 will be changed on the rising edge of MR001. 
LD MR000
MOV #3 DM0
LDP MR001
ANB CR6704
ANB CR6705
SET CR6604
MOV DM0 CM6000
;<h1/>When this change fails, R500 will be ON. 
LDP CR6704
MPS
ANB CR6705
RES CR6604
MPP
AND CR6705
OUT R500
END
ENDH
